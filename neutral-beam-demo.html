Stage 1 of 6

Ion extraction

Positive ions are extracted from the RF plasma source. Radio-frequency energy ionizes the source gas; extraction electrodes draw the ions out to form a beam.

Tokamak geometry and magnetic field lines are simplified. Neutral atoms are unaffected by the bending magnet; residual charged particles are deflected into a beam dump. The actual component layout and beam trajectories vary by device.

Source: Hopf, C., Bachmann, C., Fellinger, T., Franke, T., Gliss, C., Härtl, T., Heinemann, B., & Orozco, G. (2025). Neutral Beam Injection for a tokamak-based Volumetric Neutron Source. Fusion Engineering and Design, 213, 114870. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.fusengdes.2025.114870
