Ion extraction

Positive ions are extracted from the RF plasma source. Radio-frequency energy ionizes the source gas; extraction electrodes draw the ions out to form a beam.

Schematic only: the actual beamline layout, neutralization fraction, energies, and injection angle vary by device. Neutral atoms are unaffected by the bending magnet; residual charged particles are not.
