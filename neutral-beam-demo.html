Temperature is an educational display, not a sensor readout or live tokamak model. Tokamak plasma has separate electron and ion temperatures and steep spatial gradients; this control represents an illustrative central temperature equivalent (about 100 million °C is approximately 100 million K at this precision). Plasma glow is stylised—real plasma emission depends on species, density, temperature and viewing conditions, and is not simply a pink blackbody.

Source: Hopf, C., Bachmann, C., Fellinger, T., Franke, T., Gliss, C., Härtl, T., Heinemann, B., & Orozco, G. (2025). Neutral Beam Injection for a tokamak-based Volumetric Neutron Source. Fusion Engineering and Design, 213, 114870. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.fusengdes.2025.114870
